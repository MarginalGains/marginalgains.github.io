# Suihei (Photo Leveling Tool) Privacy Policy

[日本語版はこちら / Japanese version is available here](./privacy-policy.md)

Marginal Gains, Inc. ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and handles information in accordance with this Privacy Policy (this "Policy") with respect to "Suihei" (the "App"), an application for macOS that we provide. The App may be displayed under a localized name depending on the distribution store or display language.

This Policy sets out the handling specific to the App. Our [general Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy.en.md) applies to our websites and other services we provide. Matters not covered by this Policy are governed by our general Privacy Policy and applicable laws and regulations.

## 1. Our Basic Approach

The App is an on-device tool for correcting the tilt of photographs (rotation and cropping). It is **designed so that it does not collect or transmit your personal information**. No account registration is required to use the App, and the photographs and other data the App processes are processed and stored solely on your device. The App does not make any internet connection in order to operate.

## 2. Personal Information We Collect

We do not collect your personal information through the App. Specifically:

- **Photographs and image data**: Photographs and images loaded into the App are processed solely on your device for the purpose of correction (tilt estimation, rotation, cropping, and export). They are never transmitted to our servers or any other external destination. The App uses a non-destructive design that does not modify the original files; corrected results are written only to the output location you specify.
- **Editing state and settings data**: Editing sessions (correction values, crop areas, etc.) and app settings are stored within the App's area on your device (such as the Application Support folder). We do not obtain this data.
- **File access permissions**: The App runs under the macOS App Sandbox and accesses only the folders and files you select. To enable automatic restoration after a restart, the App stores on your device the access permissions you have granted (security-scoped bookmarks).

## 3. Obtaining and Purchasing the App (via Apple)

The App is distributed through the Mac App Store operated by Apple Inc. ("Apple"). Obtaining, paying for, and otherwise transacting for the App is conducted through Apple, and your Apple Account, payment information, and purchase history are handled by Apple. **We do not receive any information that personally identifies a purchaser (such as name, email address, or payment information).**

We may receive from Apple statistical sales information that does not identify individuals (such as the number of sales by country or region and revenue summaries), but this does not allow us to identify any individual customer.

For information on how Apple handles information, please refer to Apple's privacy policy.

- Apple Privacy Policy: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

## 4. Analytics, Crash Reporting, and Advertising

- The App **does not incorporate any third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, or advertising SDKs**. The App does not display advertisements.
- Only if you have consented, through the features of the operating system (Apple), to share diagnostics and usage data, Apple may collect crash and usage information, and we may review it in an **aggregated and anonymized form**. Such information is not transmitted by the App itself but is handled under Apple's control. You can change your sharing preference at any time in your device settings (e.g., Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

## 5. Disclosure to Third Parties

Because we do not collect your personal information through the App, we hold no personal information to disclose to third parties. As described above, information relating to transactions made through Apple is handled by Apple, and diagnostic information shared with your consent is handled by Apple, each in accordance with its own privacy policy.

## 6. International Data Transfers

We are located in Japan. Because the App does not transmit personal information off your device, no international transfer of data by us occurs. Information relating to transactions and diagnostics handled through Apple may be processed on servers located in various countries in accordance with Apple's privacy policy.

## 7. Security Measures

By processing your data on-device and not transmitting it externally, the App structurally reduces the risk of information leakage. We strive to take necessary and appropriate security measures in developing and distributing the App.

## 8. Your Rights

Because we do not hold your personal data through the App, there is in principle no personal data subject to requests for disclosure, correction, or deletion directed to us. Data created within the App (editing sessions, settings, output files, etc.) can be deleted by you by deleting the relevant files or by uninstalling the App.

Depending on the laws of your country or region (such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)), you may have rights such as the rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, objection, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. We will respond to requests regarding information we hold in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. For information handled by Apple, please contact Apple.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age at which parental consent is required under the laws of their country or region. Because the App does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. Supported Platforms and Scope of This Policy

The App is currently provided for macOS. If we provide the App or an equivalent app for iPad (iPadOS), Windows, or other platforms in the future, we will strive to maintain the basic approach of this Policy—processing data on-device and not transmitting it externally. If any change in handling arises, we will revise this Policy and announce its content.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy as necessary, such as in response to changes in laws and regulations or in the specifications of the App. If we make material changes, we will announce them on our website or on the App's store page.
